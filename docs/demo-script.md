# 60-second walkthrough — voiceover script

**0:00–0:08 — Hook.** Navigation gets you to the door. Then you circle the block for five minutes. What if Maps told you, before you arrived, whether you'd find a spot?

**0:08–0:18 — Problem.** The last minutes of a city trip are spent hunting for parking, and drivers get no signal until they're there.

**0:18–0:40 — Demo.** This is live City of Melbourne sensor data: 275 blocks, colour-coded. I tap my destination on Lonsdale Street: "Parking hard", zero of five bays free. The card suggests a better bet 285 metres away, and one tap takes me there: "Parking likely." Switch to Live and it pulls the city's feed right now. It works on mobile too.

**0:40–0:52 — Result.** In real data, 62 of 275 blocks were hard while the city average was 43% free. And 42% of sensors were silent for a week, so data quality is the first problem.

**0:52–0:60 — Next.** I'd A/B test it in Melbourne with one North Star: minutes from arrival to parked.
